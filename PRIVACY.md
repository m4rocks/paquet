# Privacy policy

Paquet has no accounts, no ads and no cookies. Here is everything that
touches your data.

## Analytics

Paquet uses [Vercel Web Analytics](https://vercel.com/docs/analytics/privacy-policy)
to count page views. It does not use cookies and does not track you across
sites or sessions. It records aggregated information such as the page you
visited, the referring site, your country, and your browser, operating system
and device type.

## Hosting

Paquet is hosted on [Vercel](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy). Like any
host, Vercel processes your IP address and request details in order to serve
the site and keep it secure. Paquet does not store or look at them.

## On your device

Paquet stores a few things in your browser, and only on your device:

- your theme preference (light or dark)
- a cache of the site's files, kept by a service worker so Paquet loads fast
  and shows an offline page when you are not connected

You can clear both from your browser settings at any time.

## Apps listed on Paquet

Paquet is a directory. When you open an app, you leave Paquet and the app's own
privacy policy applies. Paquet links to it on the app's page when the app
provides one.

## Questions
